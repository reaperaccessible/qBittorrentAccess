qBittorrentAccess - Changelog

Version française

Version 1.00

First version of qBittorrentAccess, based on qBittorrent 5.2.3: a BitTorrent client fully usable with a screen reader (NVDA, JAWS, Narrator) and from the keyboard alone. Free.

New: the torrent list can be reached from the keyboard and is read column by column. The Left and Right arrows read each detail of the torrent (size, progress, status, speeds, time left…), the Up and Down arrows then stay in the same column, Home and End go to the Name column and to the last column. The value you read stays on the braille display. The ends of the list are announced ("First" and "Last"). The Space bar starts or stops the torrent, Ctrl+Enter opens its folder, Ctrl+Shift+C copies its magnet link.

New: shortcuts to every part of the program. Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+5 for the lists (all, downloading, completed, seeding, errors), Ctrl+6, Ctrl+7 and Ctrl+8 for search, RSS feeds and the log, Ctrl+Shift+1 to Ctrl+Shift+6 for the torrent properties, F6 and Shift+F6 to move from one area to another. Ctrl+Shift+G says the overall status (speeds, connection, DHT). Ctrl+Shift+H shows the list of all shortcuts. Ctrl+Comma opens the options.

New: every window, option, button and menu has a clear name. Windows say what they are for and which keys to use. Information lists (general properties, statistics, add window) are read line by line, and Ctrl+C copies the value. Context menus put the focus on the first item and point out their submenus. Three-state check boxes (category, tags, sequential download) are announced checked, not checked or partially checked.

New: values are spelled out (sizes, speeds, durations, "infinity", "not available"), never abbreviated.

New: the Trackers, Peers and Content lists of the properties, and the content of the add window, are read column by column like the torrent list; in the trees, Plus and Minus open and close folders.

New: in the Add torrent links window (Ctrl+Shift+O), a Links list shows each link; when links are taken from the clipboard, the focus is put on this list; Ctrl+V announces the number of pasted links.

New: on the first start, qBittorrentAccess takes a copy of the torrents and settings of qBittorrent if it is installed, without changing qBittorrent.

New: torrent search works right after installation. Python is built into the program (nothing to install) and about fifteen checked public search plugins are installed on the first start. Result names are made readable for speech and braille: spaces instead of dots and underscores, encoded or garbled characters turned back into plain text. The results are read column by column with the Left and Right arrows, exactly like the torrent list.

New: user manual in English and French on F1, changelog on Ctrl+F1. Complete French translation.
